About the expo

Hosted by the Institute for Information Industry (AI Research Institute) and the Greater South New Silicon Valley Promotion Office under the "Healthy Taiwan" and "Balanced Taiwan" visions, the expo centres on industry matchmaking and application deployment — a business-matching area, Demo Days, and five themed interactive zones spanning smart care, rehabilitation, clinical diagnosis and industry empowerment.
